# Deploying DRCV (Render, free)

1. Put this folder on GitHub (new repository, upload all files).
2. Go to https://render.com, sign in with GitHub.
3. Click **New +  ->  Blueprint**, choose the repository, click **Apply**.
4. Wait about 10 minutes for the first build. Your site opens at `https://drcv-xxxx.onrender.com`.
5. Click **Launch Demo**.

Notes: the free plan sleeps after 15 minutes idle (first load then takes ~30-60 s); the free database expires after 30 days; files uploaded on the free plan are lost when the service restarts (demo data is re-created automatically).
